ABOUT INTERNCONNECT

Supporting Students Through Real-World Experience

InternConnect is a web-based university internship management system that connects students, companies, faculty members and administrators through one organised platform.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Internship Management Easier

Finding and managing an internship can be difficult when information is spread across different platforms. InternConnect brings the complete process into one place.

Students can discover opportunities and track applications. Companies can publish internship posts and connect with suitable candidates. Faculty members can monitor student progress, while administrators manage the entire platform.

Our Main Goal

To create a simple, transparent and organised connection between academic learning and professional experience.

OUR USERS

One Platform for Every Participant

01

Students

Students can create profiles, browse internships, submit applications and track their progress.

02

Companies

Companies can publish internship opportunities and find students with suitable skills.

03

Faculty Advisors

Faculty members can monitor internship activities and provide academic guidance.

04

Administrators

Administrators can manage users, companies, internships and application records.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

A Better Internship Experience

Organised Information

Internship details, applications and user information can be managed in one system.

Clear Communication

Students, companies and faculty members can stay connected throughout the internship process.

Progress Monitoring

Application status and internship progress can be monitored more effectively.
